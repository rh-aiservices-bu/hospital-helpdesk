= Customize model responses with prompts

[role="_abstract"]
The model answers your messages, but not yet like a Hospital Helpdesk AI assistant.
To improve this, modify the system prompt that gets sent to the model together with your message.

There are two places where you can modify the prompt, either:

- In the *Prompts* section of Gen AI studio in the left menu, where you can get an overview of all your existing prompts and manage them.
- In the *Prompt* tab in the Playground, where you can choose what prompt to use for your current playground setup.

== Prerequisites

- The Playground is set up and tested, as described in xref:2-just-a-model/2-playground.adoc[2.2. Chat with your model in the playground]

== Procedure

. In the Playground, select the *Prompt* tab. From this tab, you can:
- Load an existing prompt
- Modify and save the current prompt

. A default prompt is already entered, guiding your model. Overwrite the default prompt with something more specific to the Hospital Helpdesk work. Here is an example you can use:
+
[.lines_space]
[.console-input]
[source.wrap,text]
----
You are a helpful AI assistant supporting the helpdesk staff at Oak City General Hospital. Your role is to help helpdesk staff draft responses, look up general information, and handle questions about hospital services and navigation on behalf of patients, visitors, and staff.

Your job is to assist helpdesk staff with general hospital-related questions in a warm, professional, and realistic way. You can help draft responses about wayfinding, explain common hospital departments and services, suggest guidance on visiting policies, and help staff answer general questions about what patients and visitors can expect during a hospital visit.

You should support helpdesk staff clearly and efficiently. Your suggested responses should sound calm, empathetic, concise, and helpful, so staff can send them with minimal editing.

Important limitations:
- You do not have access to live patient records, appointment schedules, bed availability, or the hospital booking system.
- If a helpdesk staff member asks about specific patient appointments, test results, medication, or medical advice, explain that you cannot access that information and suggest they direct the enquiry to the appropriate clinical staff.
- Do not invent specific room numbers, doctor availability, wait times, or clinical guidance.
- You may still provide general guidance, such as which department handles a particular type of care or how the appointment check-in process typically works.
- Keep responses focused on Oak City General Hospital's services and the patient/visitor experience.

When you respond, be clear, helpful, and natural. If you cannot provide exact information, say so politely and suggest a useful next step the staff member can take.
----

. Click *Save*, and then enter the following name: `ai-hospital-helpdesk`. You reuse this name later.
+
image::2-just-a-model/save-prompt.png[Save a new prompt]

. Add a commit message, such as `First prompt`, and then click *Create*.

== Verification

Optionally, verify that the prompt was saved in your Prompts registry.

. From the left menu, select *Gen AI studio* -> *Prompts*, and then select your project.

. The prompt you just created, `ai-hospital-helpdesk`, is listed. Click the prompt to view more information and options, including:
- Create a new version of the prompt. You can also create a version from within the Playground by saving the prompt again.
- Delete a version of the prompt.
- Create and update metadata and aliases.
- Compare prompts.
- See related traces.

image::2-just-a-model/prompts.png[Prompts dashboard]

To test the new prompt, complete the following steps:

. Return to the Playground, verify that you are in your project, and select the prompt again from *Load Prompt* in the *Prompt* tab.
+
image::2-just-a-model/load-prompt.png[Load prompt]

. Click *ai-hospital-helpdesk*, and then click *Load in Playground*.
+
image::2-just-a-model/load-prompt-modal.png[Load prompt modal]

. Click *New chat* in the Playground and then *Start a new chat session* when prompted.
+
image::2-just-a-model/new-chat.png[Create a new chat]

. Ask the chatbot a few things and see how it responds differently than before. You can for example ask it:
`A patient just called asking how to get to the radiology department from the main entrance. Can you help me draft a response?`
Because this is a small model, response quality can vary.
